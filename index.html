Kàlẹ́ndà

A personal productivity assistant that manages Google and Outlook calendars through the messaging apps you already use. Schedule, reschedule and review events from Telegram or Slack, and receive reminders ahead of every commitment. WhatsApp support is coming soon.

I designed and built the full product: the LangGraph agent, Google and Microsoft sign-in, Telegram and Slack integrations, an account dashboard for connecting calendars, and deployment on Render. Any Slack workspace can install it.
